Savi - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04 July 2025
Jurisdiction: Ireland

At Savi, protecting your privacy is one of our highest priorities. This policy outlines what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Info: Username, email address, age verification
  • Usage Data: Activity logs (for safety and moderation only)
  • Device Info: Browser type, IP addrss (for security and support)

We do not collect unnecessary personal data. We use only what is needed to provide a safe environment for our users.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide a safe and functional service
  • To detect and prevent harmful behavior
  • To ensure users fall within the eligible age range
  • To comply with legal and safety obligations

3. No Sale or Sharing of Data

Savi will NEVER, under any circumstances, sell, rent, or share any personal data of any user with advertisers, third parties, or external services. Your data stays strictly within the Savi ecosystem.

4. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for moderation, legal compliance, or platform functionality. Deleted accounts are permanently removed after a reasonable retention period for safety checks.

5. Parental Rights and Controls

Parents or guardians may request access to their child’s data or request account deletion by contacting savi-accountdeletion@lns.dolphinnetwork.ie with appropriate verification.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Savi is fully committed to privacy protections under COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation for Kids). No personal information is collected beyond what is needed to operate safely.

7. Security

We use encryption, access controls, and strict internal protocols to protect all data. All moderation is performed by trained, vetted staff.

If you have questions about these policies, please contact us at savi@legal.dolphinnetwork.ie or visit https://legal.dolphinnetwork.ie.
